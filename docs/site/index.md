# Tirkit

Tirkit is an open-source, self-hostable platform for **observing** and **evaluating** LLM
applications: every kind of model (text, vision, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, small local models),
traced, scored, compared with confidence intervals, and gated in CI. Its LLM judge reports how far it
can be trusted.

- **Observe:** traces with images and audio, sessions, users, cost, latency, dashboards, alerts.
- **Evaluate:** datasets mined from traces, rule and LLM-judge scorers, experiments, side-by-side
  comparison with 95% CIs, human annotation, online evals, a CI gate.
- **Iterate:** versioned prompts with deploy labels and a playground.
- **Trust:** judge-reliability reports (kappa, position-swap flip rate, length bias) per rubric version.
